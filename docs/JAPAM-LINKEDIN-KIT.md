## Long description (LinkedIn “About” — full)

Japam reimagines daily **japa** (mantra repetition) for the smartphone era—without replacing temple worship or family puja traditions.

**How it works:** Players swap deity-colored gems on a Candy Crush–style board. Every match plays authentic **mantra audio** and adds to personal japa totals. Progress syncs to the cloud when you sign in with Google.

**Solo practice**
- **General Japa** — all deities on one board  
- **Iṣṭa Devatā Japa** — focus on one deity  
- **1,000 levels** across 100 Sanātana-themed episodes  
- **Japa dashboard** — lifetime counts per deity, PDF export (name, gotram, mantra, count)  
- **Specials:** Pushpa Aradhana (digital flower offering), 108-japa mode, weekly streak (IST)

**Community & temples**
- **Japa Marathons** — discover events by State → District → City → Area; Pro members join and compete on leaderboards  
- **Maha Japa Yagnas** — collective chanting goals (e.g. lakhs of japas) run by admins and temple priests  
- **Apavarga** — Pro/Premium community app: chat with priests, status, appointments, groups  

**22 playable deities** including Rama, Shiva, Ganesh, Krishna, Hanuman, Lakshmi, Durga, Saraswati, Venkateswara, Shanmukha, Navagraha (Surya, Guru, Shani, Rahu, Ketu), and more—with mantra text and audio per deity.

**Made for India:** 23 language UI options (Telugu default), Cashfree payments, installable **PWA** (Add to Home Screen), offline support, 20-minute wellbeing break reminders.

**Pro / Premium:** Unlock advanced levels and marathons; Premium dakshina supports the mission and unlocks Apavarga.

Japam connects digital devotion to real temples—priests create marathons; devotees chant anywhere and still participate in sankalpa.

🔗 https://japam.digital

## Complete feature list

### Core game
- Match-3 puzzle gameplay (swap gems, cascades, move limits)
- Mantra audio on every qualifying match
- **General Japa** — all deity matches count  
- **Deity-specific / Iṣṭa Devatā** — only chosen deity counts  
- **1,000 levels**, **100 episodes** (10 levels each), Sanātana episode names  
- Free play through early levels; **Pro (Dakshina)** unlocks level 6+  
- Stars, level map, pause/resume with cloud save  
- Power boosters (roadmap: Namaskaram, per-deity offerings, free swap, 5-match flower clear)

### Deities & audio
- **22 playable deities** on boards and menus  
- Mantra strings per deity (including longer forms for 3/4/5 matches)  
- Dedicated artwork + in-game face crops per deity  
- Guru-reserved deities (Sai Baba, Bramhamgaaru) held for future release—not in public play

### Japa tracking
- Lifetime japa per deity + grand total  
- Japa Count dashboard (`/japa`)  
- PDF download with mantra, count, name, gotram, mobile, optional handwriting image  
- 20-minute continuous play reminder  

### Special modes (`/specials`)
- **Pushpa Aradhana** — flower-offering mini-game; separate leaderboard; upachāra ritual steps; Ganesh free, others Pro
